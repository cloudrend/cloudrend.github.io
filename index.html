Rules & Regulations

Chat and Community Rules

Be Respectful:

Harassment, bullying, toxicity, toxic arguments, or any form of hate speech will result in an immediate permanent ban. Treat everyone with kindness.

No Spamming:

Avoid flooding the text chat with repetitive messages, excessive capital letters, or walls of random characters.

Appropriate Content and Language:

Keep the chat environment clean and family-friendly. Avoid excessive swearing or crude expressions.

No NSFW Content:

Any topics, references, images, or jokes related to NSFW/adult material are strictly prohibited across all text channels.

No Advertising:

Do not promote other Discord servers, external Minecraft servers, or unapproved website links without direct approval from the management.

Staff Discretion and Privacy:

Do not argue with staff members or dispute punishments in public chat. If you have an issue, query, or complaint, please use our private Ticket System.

In-Game Rules

No Griefing:

Do not destroy, modify, or steal from other players' builds or containers without explicit permission.

No Hacking or Cheating:

The use of clients, unapproved mods, X-ray packs, fly hacks, or auto-clickers is strictly prohibited. Keep the playing field level!

Respect Personal Space:

Do not build within 100 blocks of another player’s base unless you are confirmed teammates. Give your neighbors room to breathe!

No Lag Machines:

Avoid constructing massive un-switchable redstone clocks, overpopulated mob farms, or devices built with the intention of slowing down the server.

PvP Consent Required:

Do not attack or kill other players unless both parties explicitly agree to a fight, or you are inside a designated PvP zone.

Enforcement and Punishment Policy

We operate on a strict three-strike warning system before permanent bans are issued for minor and major infractions. Infractions reset at the end of the season.

Minor Infractions

Swearing, minor spam, light toxicity, or building too close to neighbors.

  • 1st Offense: Official Warning
  • 2nd Offense: 1-Hour Mute / Kick
  • 3rd Offense: 24-Hour Temp-Ban

Major Infractions

Griefing, stealing, server disrespect, or continuous harassment.

  • 1st Offense: 3-Day Server Ban
  • 2nd Offense: 7-Day Server Ban
  • 3rd Offense: Permanent Ban

Zero Tolerance

Hacking, X-ray, lag machines, advertising, or NSFW distribution.

  • Action: Immediate & Permanent Ban from Minecraft and Discord without warning.
Appeals: If you feel your punishment was unjust, open a Ban Appeal Ticket on Discord. Decisions made on Zero Tolerance rules are final.
